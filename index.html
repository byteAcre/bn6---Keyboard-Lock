Keyboard Lock Privacy Policy

Keyboard Lock, by ByteAcre · Effective 16 November 2026 (version 1.1)

Keyboard Lock is a Windows app that locks your keyboard (and, with Keyboard Lock Pro, your mouse and touchpad) so you can clean them, or keep a cat or a child from typing. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does with it. In short: Keyboard Lock never records, stores or sends what you type, and it does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything stays on your PC.

How the app uses your keyboard and mouse

  • Keyboard. To work, Keyboard Lock watches key presses on your PC (a Windows "keyboard hook"). It uses them only to notice its own lock shortcut (for example Ctrl + Alt + Shift + L) and, while the keyboard is locked, to block keys and notice the unlock combination (for example holding Ctrl + Alt + U). Key presses are never written to a file, never kept after they have been checked, and never sent anywhere.
  • Cat guard (optional, Pro). If you turn it on, the app looks at the timing of key presses (how many keys go down at the same moment) to recognise a cat or a child mashing the keys. For this it keeps the times of the last few key presses in memory for about 1.5 seconds. It does not keep the text you type.
  • Mouse and touchpad (Pro). While you have locked them, the app blocks mouse movement, clicks and scrolling. It does not record where you move or click.
  • Game keys (optional, Pro). If you turn it on, the app checks about once a second which window is in front: its size, whether it has a title bar, and the file name of its program (for example "game.exe"), to know whether a game is in front. While one is, it blocks the Windows key (and Alt + Tab, if you chose that). This information is used at that moment and not kept; only the program names you add yourself are saved in your settings.
  • Schedule (optional, Pro). The app locks the keyboard at the times you set. Those times are saved in your settings.

Pro add-on and purchase

Keyboard Lock is free. "Keyboard Lock Pro" is an optional one-time add-on sold in the Microsoft Store. Purchases are made in Microsoft's own purchase window; ByteAcre never sees your payment details. To know whether you own Pro, the app asks the Microsoft Store on your PC for its license. That check is handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; Keyboard Lock does not send anything about you with it.

Information stored on your PC

Keyboard Lock saves the following only on your PC, in the app's own data folder:

  • your settings: the lock shortcut, the unlock combination, the cleaning time, the safety time limit and the cat guard, game keys and schedule options (including the program names and times you add);
  • with Pro: how many times cat guard locked the keyboard ("paw attacks stopped"), and, unless you turn it off, a history of locks: when each lock started, how long it lasted, what was locked, how it was started and how it ended. The history never contains the keys you pressed. "Clear history" in the app deletes it;
  • whether you owned Pro at the last license check, so the app works offline;
  • a small log file of what the app did (for example "Locked: Lock keyboard" or "Unlocked: Safety"), which helps with troubleshooting. It never contains the keys you pressed, and it never leaves your PC unless you choose to send it to us.

Uninstalling the app removes its data folder.

What the app does not do

  • It does not record, store or send keystrokes, text, mouse movements or clicks.
  • It does not send any information to ByteAcre or anyone else. Its only online activity is the Microsoft Store license and purchase check described above.
  • It does not take screenshots or look at what is on your screen (game keys only checks the size and program of the window in front, as described above).
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not access your files, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Start with Windows

With Keyboard Lock Pro you can choose to start the app when you sign in to Windows (it is off until you turn it on). You can turn it off in the app or in Windows Settings > Apps > Startup.

Children

Keyboard Lock is often used by parents so a child can watch a video without pressing keys. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
